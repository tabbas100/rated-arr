The line on a title page that says where the title stands in your library.

**Use** directly under the details line of a title you have.

**Provide** one sentence in `badge` type: "In your library · Ready to watch", or "In your library · 301 of 457 episodes" for a show.

**Look.** `ready` text on `ready-wash` with a `ready` outline, `radius-pill`, 30px high.

**In the app:** `@id/status` in `activity_detail.xml`, `drawable/badge_status`.
